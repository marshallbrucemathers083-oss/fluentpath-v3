# FluentPath V2 design direction

- Identity: a practical, welcoming English study path from A1 to C2.
- Audience: independent learners who want steady, clearly structured progress.
- Personality: calm, encouraging, precise, and editorial.
- Palette: warm paper and ink, with evergreen for progress and persimmon for action. These colors separate reading surfaces, learning progress, and the next useful action.
- Type: a readable humanist sans for controls and lesson text, paired with a restrained book serif for editorial headings. The contrast connects app utility with language learning.
- Layout: curriculum first. A visible path and lesson content take priority over metrics or decorative cards.
- Motion: short transitions clarify navigation, feedback, and progress changes. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- Assets: no invented people, testimonials, logos, or statistics. Use the product name as the wordmark and initials for member avatars.
